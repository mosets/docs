# Access Key

A Mosets Access Key authenticates your site with the Mosets Update server and allows your site to check and update your Mosets Tree installation through the Joomla Update system.

## Get an Access Key
   
To get an access key, you need to have an active Mosets Tree subscription.
- **Purchase a new subscription**: If you do not have an existing subscription, you can purchase one at https://www.mosets.com/buy/tree/
- **Renew your subscription**: If you have an expired subscription, you must renew your subscription. You can renew your subscription by logging in to your Mosets account. There is a link beside your Subscription ID where you can purchase a renewal.

If you have an active subscription:
1. Login to your Mosets account: https://www.mosets.com/login.
2. Click on your **Subscription ID**.
3. Click "**Generate New Access Key**". 
    - Note: If you have an existing access key, you can use it.

## Using an Access Key

To use an Access Key on your Mosets Tree website, ensure you have version 3.9.6 or later.

1. Login to your site's back-end.
2. Go to "**Mosets Tree -> My Subscription**".
3. Copy the Access Key and paste it into the "**Access Key**" field.
4. Click the "**Verify**" button.

This verifies your access key with the Mosets Update server. If your subscription is active, you'll receive a confirmation message.
